Practical Arabic · Overview

Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will master essential competencies for humanitarian fieldwork in Arabic-speaking regions.

Conversational Fluency

Initiate and sustain basic greetings and status exchanges confidently with locals and colleagues.

Self-Presentation

Confidently introduce your identity, origin, and professional humanitarian background.

Domain-Specific Vocabulary

Utilize essential humanitarian, aid, and field-work terminology accurately in context.

Sentence Construction

Apply foundational Arabic grammar patterns to formulate your own descriptive sentences on the field.

Practical Arabic · Module 1

Greetings & Status

السلام عليكم

Assalamu Alaikum

Peace be upon you

Breakdown: Al-Salam (Peace) + 'Ala (Upon) + -kum (You)

كيف حالك؟

Kaifa haluk?

How are you?

Breakdown: Kaifa (How) + Hal (Condition) + -ka (Your)

أنا بخير، الحمد لله

Ana bikhair, Alhamdulillah

I am fine, praise be to Allah

Breakdown: Ana (I) + Bi-khair (In good) + Praise to Allah

Practical Arabic · Module 2

Personal Identity

ما اسمك؟

Ma ismuk?

What is your name?

Ma (What) + Ism (Name) + -uk (Your)

اسمي ...

Ismi ...

My name is ...

Ism (Name) + -i (My)

من أين أنت؟

Min aina anta?

Where are you from?

Min (From) + Aina (Where) + Anta (You)

أنا من ...

Ana min ...

I am from ...

Ana (I) + Min (From)

Practical Arabic · Module 3

Humanitarian Action

أعمل في المجال الإنساني

A'mal fil-majalil-insani

I work in the humanitarian field

A'mal (I work) + Fi (in) + Al-Majal (the field) + Al-Insani (humanitarian)

نحن نساعد المحتاجين

Nahnu nusa'idul muhtajin

We help people in need

Nahnu (We) + Nusa'id (help) + Al-Muhtajin (people in need)

يحتاج الناس إلى الطعام والماء

Yahtajunnas ilatta'am wal-maa

People need food and water

Yahtaju (Need) + Al-Nas (people) + Ila (to) + Al-Ta'am (food) + Wa (and) + Al-Ma' (water)
